# Paper and symbol of a note

Every note can sit on its own paper: **blank, lined, grid or dots**. This one is on grid paper. Choose the paper under "More actions" (the "…" at the top of the note).

![Choosing the paper](shots/paper.webp)

Which paper new notes start on is set under **Settings › My account › Appearance**.

## A symbol before the title

A faint placeholder stands before the title. A click opens the same choice as for folders: more than 250 symbols with search. The symbol also shows in the list of notes, in the inbox and in the tabs.

![Choosing a note's symbol](shots/note-icon.webp)
