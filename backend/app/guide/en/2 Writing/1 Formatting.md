# Formatting

Above the note sits the toolbar: text style (heading, subheading, body text), bold, italic, highlighter, lists, checklist, table, link and more. It stays on top while you scroll.

![The toolbar above a note](shots/toolbar.webp)

## The format bar at the selection

When you select text, a small bar with the essentials appears right beside it: **bold**, *italic*, underline, ~~strikethrough~~, ==highlighter==, link and "Clear formatting".

![The format bar above selected text](shots/selection-bar.webp)

## A few shortcuts

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| Ctrl+B | bold |
| Ctrl+I | italic |
| Ctrl+U | underline |
| Ctrl+Alt+K | link |
| Ctrl+Z | undo |

On a Mac use ⌘ instead of Ctrl. The highlighter comes in five colours: honey, green, blue, rose and violet.
