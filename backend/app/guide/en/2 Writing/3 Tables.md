# Tables

Insert a table with the table symbol in the toolbar. It starts as wide as the text, and the columns share the space.

![A table with its menu](shots/table.webp)

## Editing

- **"+" at the edges** adds a row or a column.
- **The table's menu** (or a right-click into a cell) inserts, moves and deletes rows and columns and turns the header row on or off.
- **Column width:** drag the border between two columns. A double click on the border fits the column to its content. Text wraps inside the cell.
