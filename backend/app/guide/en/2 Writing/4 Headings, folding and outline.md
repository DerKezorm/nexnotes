# Headings, folding and outline

Headings bring order into long notes. In the text style choose "Heading", "Subheading" or "Minor heading".

## Folding

Next to every heading sits a small arrow. A click folds the section up to the next heading of the same size. Folded stays folded, on your other devices too.

![The outline beside a note with a folded section](shots/outline.webp)

## The outline

The button **"Show or hide the outline"** lists all headings to the right of the note. A click jumps there. Below them stand the note's tags, its folder and when it was made and changed.

## Text width

**"Text width"** chooses narrow, wide or the full width. Narrow reads best; the full width helps with big tables.
