# Text recognition

nexnotes reads the text in photos, scans and PDFs. After that, search also finds words that are only on a receipt, a letter or a whiteboard.

![A photo with its recognised text beside it](shots/ocr.webp)

## How it works

1. Attach a photo or PDF to a note.
2. nexnotes recognises the text in the background. With many pages this takes a while.
3. Open the picture with **"Open large"**. There **"Show text"** shows the recognised text, to copy or to insert into the note.

## Good to know

- Which languages are recognised is set by the operator under Settings › Server › Text recognition.
- Locked notes in the vault are not read.
- The pictures of this guide are left out of text recognition, so that search does not find everything twice.
