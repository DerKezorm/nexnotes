# Attachments, pictures, sketches and recordings

Drag photos, PDFs, videos and other files into the note, paste them with Ctrl+V or choose them with **"Attach"**. A file may be as large as the operator allows (100 MB by default).

![A note with a photo and a PDF](shots/attachments.webp)

## What nexnotes makes of them

- **Pictures** stand small, medium or full width in the note. A click opens them large.
- **PDFs** show their first page and open to leaf through.
- **Videos** play right in the note.
- **Links** you paste become a card with a picture and a title, if you like.

## Sketches and recordings

Sketches and voice recordings are made in the iPhone and iPad app. In the browser you see sketches as pictures and play recordings; the recording's text runs along, and a click on a word jumps there.

## Every attachment at a glance

The smart folder **"Attachments"** shows every file of your notes as a gallery, filtered by kind and sorted by size if you like.
