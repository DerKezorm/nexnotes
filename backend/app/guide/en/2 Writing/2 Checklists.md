# Checklists

Start a checklist with the tick symbol in the toolbar. Every line becomes an item to tick off. Indent a line to make a sub-item.

![A shopping list with items done](shots/checklist.webp)

## Done items to the bottom

Below the checklist turn on **"Done to the bottom"**. Ticked items then move to the end, and what is open stays on top. In the list of notes nexnotes shows how many items are done, such as "2 of 5".
