# New note, title and inbox

Make a new note with **"New note"** above the list. It goes into the folder you have open; without a folder it lands in the inbox.

![A new note with the cursor in its title](shots/new-note.webp)

## Every note has a title

The title stands above the text and is required, so that you recognise the note in lists and in search. At most 120 characters. If you just start with the text, nexnotes suggests a title from the first words when you close the note. A suggestion carries the mark **"Check title"** until you confirm or change it.

## The inbox

The inbox is the place for quick things: what you share from another app, send through a connection or make without a folder waits here. A small mark says where it came from. **"File"** takes a note to its folder, or drag it onto a folder in the sidebar.

![The inbox with notes and where they came from](shots/inbox.webp)

The inbox is always there. It cannot be renamed or deleted.
