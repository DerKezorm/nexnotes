# Folders and symbols

Make folders with **+** next to "Folders" in the sidebar. Folders can live in folders, as deep as you like. A name has at most 60 characters.

![The dialog "Symbol and color" of a folder](shots/folder-style.webp)

## Symbol and colour

Every folder carries a symbol, the folder by default. Under **"…" › Symbol and color** you pick one of more than 250 symbols (with search) and one of twelve colours, or your own as `#rrggbb`. So you spot "Recipes" or "Holidays" at a glance.

## Arranging

- **Drag:** drag folders and notes onto another folder with the mouse. Dropped between two folders, the order changes.
- **Menu "…"** or right-click: new subfolder, rename, move, share, export, move to trash.
- **Keyboard:** the arrow keys walk through the folders, F2 renames.

A deleted folder takes its subfolders and notes into the trash, and comes back from there complete.
