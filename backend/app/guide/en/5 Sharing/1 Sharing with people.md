# Sharing with people

Share notes and whole folders with other accounts on this server. In the menu of the note or folder choose **"Share …"**.

![The dialog for sharing a note](shots/share.webp)

## How

1. Type a name or an e-mail address and choose the person.
2. Choose the right: **read** or **edit**.
3. "Done".

## Good to know

- What you share stays yours. Only you decide who sees it; others cannot share it further.
- A shared folder shares everything in it, including notes added later.
- What others share with you is in the smart folder **"Shared with me"**.
- If the person is not on the server yet, the operator can invite them.
