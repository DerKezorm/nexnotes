# Writing at the same time

Several people can write in a shared note at the same time, and so can you in two windows. Everybody sees the others' changes at once. There are no conflict copies and no "who saved last?".

![Two people writing in the same note](shots/presence.webp)

## How you notice

- Small pictures at the top of the note show who is there right now.
- The others' cursors carry their names.
- Below the title it says when and by whom the note was last changed.

If the connection drops for a moment, just keep writing. nexnotes sends your changes as soon as the server is back.
