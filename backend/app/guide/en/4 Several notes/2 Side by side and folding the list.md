# Side by side and folding the list

Two notes side by side help when you copy, compare, or need a list next to a plan.

![Two notes side by side](shots/split.webp)

## How

- In the menu of a note choose **"Open beside"**, or drag a tab to the right edge and drop it.
- The active side has a honey frame, the other one is dimmed. The toolbar at the top works on the active side.
- The border between the two sides can be moved.
- **"Show one note only"** ends side by side.

## More room: fold the list

Side by side, **"«"** at the top of the list folds the list of notes away. A narrow strip with **"»"** stays at the edge and opens it again. If the window is too narrow for everything, the list lies as a drawer over the left note.
