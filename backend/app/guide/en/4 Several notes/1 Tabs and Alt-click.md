# Tabs and Alt-click

Up to six notes stay open as tabs. When you change the folder, only the list changes; your tabs stay where they are, as in a mail program.

![Several notes as tabs](shots/tabs.webp)

## Opening tabs

- **Alt+click** (⌥-click on a Mac) on a note in the list opens it in a new tab.
- In the menu of a note: **"Open in a new tab"**. If it is open already, it says "Go to tab".
- A plain click replaces the active tab.

## Shortcuts

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| Alt+Shift+1 to 6 | choose a tab |
| Alt+Shift+arrow right or left | next or previous tab |

With six tabs open, the oldest one closes, and nexnotes tells you which.
