# Tags

Just write a tag into the text: a `#` and a word, like #guide in this note. While you type, nexnotes suggests the tags you already use.

![Tags in the sidebar and a note with a tag](shots/tags.webp)

## What tags can do

- Your tags stand in the sidebar. A click shows every note with it.
- The menu of a tag renames or deletes it, in all your notes at once. If the new name exists, the two are merged.
- Search narrows down to a tag, and a smart folder can collect every note with (or without) a tag.

This note carries #guide. Click it in the sidebar to see the list.
