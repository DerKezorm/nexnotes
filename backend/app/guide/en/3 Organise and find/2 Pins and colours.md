# Pins and colours

Important things belong on top. In the menu of a note in the list (right-click or "…") choose **"Pin"**. Pinned notes stand at the top of every list under "Pinned". This guide pinned "Welcome" for you.

![The menu of a note with Pin and Color](shots/pin-color.webp)

## Colours

In the same menu you give a note a **colour**. It shows as a mark in the list. This note is honey. Colours are good for anything you want to tell apart at a glance: private and work, urgent and some day.

A smart folder can collect all notes of one colour later.
