# Search

**Ctrl+K** (⌘K on a Mac) or the search field at the top searches all titles and texts, including those others share with you and the recognised text of photos and PDFs.

![Search with results and filters](shots/search.webp)

## Tips

- **Several words** find notes that contain all of them.
- **"Words in quotes"** find exactly that sequence.
- **Filters** narrow down to a folder, a tag or a period.
- Results in an attachment say "in attachment:" and name the file.

Save a search you need often with **"Save as smart folder"**. Locked notes in the vault are not searched.
