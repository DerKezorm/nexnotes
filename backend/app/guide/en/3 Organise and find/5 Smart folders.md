# Smart folders

A smart folder is a saved search that looks like a folder. It collects every note that fits its conditions, whichever folder the note is in. It moves nothing: the notes stay where they are.

![A new smart folder with conditions and preview](shots/smart.webp)

## Included

You get four right away: **Open checklists**, **Recently edited**, **Shared with me** and **Attachments**. Change, rename or delete them as you like.

## Making your own

1. Click **+** next to "Smart" in the sidebar.
2. Choose conditions: text, tag, folder, period, open checklists, attachment, colour or shared.
3. Say whether **all** or **at least one** condition must fit.
4. The preview shows at once what fits right now. Then "Save".

Smart folders carry a symbol and a colour too, and can be moved in the sidebar.
