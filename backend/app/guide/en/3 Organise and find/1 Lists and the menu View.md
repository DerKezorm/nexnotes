# Lists and the menu "View"

How your lists of notes look is chosen in the menu **"View"** at the top of the list:

- **List:** title, the start of the text and the date.
- **Cards:** a little larger, with a picture.
- **Compact:** titles only, many notes at a glance.
- **Tiles:** previews in a grid.

![The menu "View" above the list of notes](shots/view-menu.webp)

The look applies to every list and follows you to every device. Sorting (last changed, created, title) and the groups by date are set per folder instead.

![Notes as tiles](shots/tiles.webp)

Under "Hints" you tick checklists, attachments and tags one by one: the list can show the progress of your checklists, say, but no tags. The same three ticks are under **Settings › Appearance** at "Note lists"; the choice holds on every device. Drag the edge of the sidebar or the list to change their width.
