# Export

Your notes are yours. **"Export …"** takes them along any time:

- **A note as Markdown:** a .md file, as a ZIP with its attachments.
- **A note as PDF:** looks like the note, with pictures.
- **A folder as ZIP:** with all subfolders, notes as Markdown with their attachments.
- **Everything:** all your own notes in their folders, plus what others share with you.

![The dialog "Export"](shots/export.webp)

Large exports run in the background. You are told when the ZIP is ready and can download it for 24 hours. Locked notes come along as titles only.
