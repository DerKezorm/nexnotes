# History and trash

## The history

nexnotes keeps earlier versions of every note. A version is made once nobody has written for a few minutes. **"History"** in the note's menu shows them by day, with the names of who wrote.

![The history of a note](shots/history.webp)

- **"Restore"** brings a version back. The current one stays in the history; nothing is lost.
- **"As a copy"** puts the old version beside it as a new note.
- The first version of this guide comes from nexnotes itself. What you change appears under your name after that.

## The trash

Deleted notes and folders stay in the trash for 90 days (the operator can change that). From there you bring them back or delete them for good.

![The trash](shots/trash.webp)
