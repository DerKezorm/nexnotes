# nextasks

If a note holds something to be done, send it as a task to your **nextasks**. The line stays in the note and gets a mark that leads to the task.

## Connecting

Under **Settings › My account › Connections › nextasks**, click **"Connect with nextasks"** and agree in nextasks. nexnotes may then read your tasks and those assigned to you, and create and change tasks, nothing else.

Afterwards you choose in the card, under **"Areas and lists"**, which areas and lists of nextasks nexnotes offers, and a **standard list** that is chosen in advance when you send.

## Sending lines

1. Put the cursor into a line or select several lines.
2. Choose **"To nextasks"** at the right of the toolbar, or **"As a nextasks task"** in the bar at the selection.
3. Choose a list, a due date if you like, then "Send".

Every line becomes a task of its own, every item of a checklist too. The mark "in nextasks" later shows where the task is and when it is due.

## The whole note as a task

In the menu of the note, **"As a nextasks task"** turns the whole note into one task: the title becomes the task, the text its notes. The note stays as it is, and the task leads back to it. If the text is longer than nextasks takes, it is cut; nexnotes tells you so beforehand.
