# nextasks

If a note holds something to be done, send the line as a task to your **nextasks**. The line stays in the note and gets a link to the task.

![Connecting nextasks under Settings › My account › Connections](shots/nextasks.webp)

## Connecting

1. In nextasks, under Settings › My account › Connections › API keys, create a key with "Read and write".
2. In nexnotes, under **Settings › My account › Connections › nextasks**, enter the address of nextasks and the key, then "Connect".

## Sending

Put the cursor into a line or select several, then **"To nextasks"** in the toolbar. Choose a list, a due date if you like, then "Send". Every item of a checklist becomes a task of its own.
