# Phone and web app

nexnotes runs in the phone's browser too. The sidebar becomes the page "Folders" there, and notes open across the whole width.

![nexnotes on a phone](shots/phone.webp)

## Installing it as an app

Put nexnotes on your home screen like an app. Under **Settings › My account › Connections › Install on this device** you see how it works in your browser, or the button for it right away:

- **Android (Chrome):** menu › "Install app" or "Add to home screen".
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** Share › "Add to Home Screen".
- **Computer (Chrome, Edge):** the install symbol in the address bar.

## Sharing on Android

Once nexnotes is installed on Android, it appears in the share menu of other apps. Text, links, photos and files then land in nexnotes with a preview and a title, in the inbox by default.

## iPhone and iPad app

For working without a network there is the iPhone and iPad app: it writes offline too and syncs as soon as the server is back.

- **Connecting:** under **Settings › My account › Connections › Devices**, **"Connect iPhone or iPad"** opens a QR code that you scan with the app. Below it is a code to type in; only a coming version of the app understands it. Without a camera, enter your server's address in the app and sign in in the browser.
- **Managing:** there you see every connected device with its model, app version and when it last checked in. You can rename a device, disconnect one or disconnect all at once. A disconnected device reaches your notes no more, at once.
- **Notifications:** under **Settings › My account › Notifications** you choose per device which notifications it gets.
- **New device:** when another device is connected to your account, you get a mail, as for a sign-in from a new browser (you can switch it off under Settings › My account › Security).
- When your account is blocked or deleted, your devices rest. Once it is unblocked or brought back, they work again without connecting anew. A device not used for a year is disconnected.

For the operator: **Settings › Server › Connections › Interfaces** has the switches **"Allow the iPhone app"** and **"Connect new devices"**. Under Server › Accounts › Manage the operator sees how many devices an account has and when they last checked in, and can disconnect them.
