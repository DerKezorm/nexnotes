# nexlore

When a quick note should become lasting knowledge, put it into your **nexlore**: as a new note with its title and text, in a space you choose.

## Connecting

Under **Settings › My account › Connections › nexlore**, click **"Connect with nexlore"** and agree in nexlore. There you choose which spaces nexnotes may put notes into. nexnotes may do nothing more in nexlore.

## Sending a note

1. In the menu of the note choose **"To nexlore"**.
2. Pick the space in nexlore and "Send".

nexnotes tells you where the note now is in nexlore. Here it stays as it is. It is a copy: what you change in nexnotes afterwards does not follow.

Good to know: locked notes in the vault never go to nexlore. Their menu has no "To nexlore".
