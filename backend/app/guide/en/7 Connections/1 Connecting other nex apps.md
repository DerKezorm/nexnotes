# Connecting other nex apps

nexnotes works together with three other nex apps: **nextasks**, **nexlore** and **nextime**. You connect them with one click. There is no key to create and no address to type.

![The card of nextasks under Settings › My account › Connections](shots/apps.webp)

## How to connect

1. Open **Settings › My account › Connections**. Each of the three apps has a card of its own there. "What would flow" already says what the connection allows.
2. Click **"Connect with …"**, for example "Connect with nextasks". The other app opens.
3. Sign in there if needed and agree once. Then you are back in nexnotes, connected.

## What you see when you agree

- The account you are signed in with in the other app, with its name and picture.
- In plain words what each app may do in the other, for example "Create and change tasks". One consent covers both directions.
- Where the app has areas: which of them the other app may use, all (including later ones) or only some.

**"Allow connecting"** agrees, **"Decline"** does not. Each app may only do what the connection is for, nothing more. If something goes wrong in the other app, you come back to your card and read there what happened.

## Once you are connected

The card shows the account you are connected with, since when, when the other app was last reached and what each side may do. If the other app does not answer right now, the card says so, and **"Check now"** asks again at once.

**"Disconnect"** ends the connection on both sides, here and in the other app. What you created yourself stays in both apps. You can disconnect in the other app too.

## For the operator

For the cards to show a button to connect, the operator enters the addresses of the other apps once: **Settings › Server › Connections › Other nex apps**. In the other app he enters the address of nexnotes the same way. Where an address is missing, the card says that the app is not set up on this server.

- If an app runs in the local network (LAN, VPN), the operator switches on **"Connections into the local network"** at the same place.
- The switch "Connections with other nex apps" turns connecting off for everybody. Existing connections then rest; nothing is deleted.

## Older connections with a key

If you connected nextasks with an API key before, that connection keeps working as it did. The card then shows "Connected with API key …". **"Connect again"** turns it into a connection made with one click; the old key ends by itself.

**"Connect by hand"**, typing an address and a key, is only shown to the operator and to accounts already connected that way.
