# nextime

For points on a meeting, **nextime** makes you a note in nexnotes with one click, with everything important about the event.

## Connecting

Under **Settings › My account › Connections › nextime**, click **"Connect with nextime"** and agree in nextime. It works the same the other way round in nextime. Before the click you choose in the card where nextime may put notes: in all folders, including later ones, or only in some.

nextime only creates notes here and reads nothing of your notes. As only nextime uses something of nexnotes, the card shows the state "Uses nexnotes", and the button to disconnect is called **"End access"**.

## A note for an event

In nextime open an event and choose **"Note in nexnotes"**. The note lands in your inbox, with the event's title, its time, its place and its notes. If you allowed nextime only some folders, it lands in one of them.

The same event never gets a second note. If you click again, nextime tells you the note is already there. nextime shows the button once it is connected with nexnotes.
